Samuel A. Edet, PhD

I am an applied economist. I use econometrics, causal inference and machine learning to study innovation, firms and financial markets, and I turn that evidence into advice for markets and policy.

More than five years at the International Finance Corporation (IFC), part of the World Bank Group, in private-market research. PhD in Applied Economics (KU Leuven). CFA Level III candidate.

Projects

Software · Python, FastAPI, CVXPY

Portfolio Construction Platform

A prototype for institutional investors. Users upload their asset data and set a mandate (long-only, long/short, leveraged, benchmark-relative or liability-driven). The app then runs optimisation, Black–Litterman, backtests, stress tests, Monte Carlo, spending policy and quarterly rebalancing. It writes an investment-committee report.
