Projects

Software · Python, FastAPI, CVXPY

Portfolio Construction Platform

A prototype for institutional investors. Users upload their asset data and set a mandate (long-only, long/short, leveraged, benchmark-relative or liability-driven). The app then runs optimisation, Black–Litterman, backtests, stress tests, Monte Carlo, spending policy and quarterly rebalancing. It writes an investment-committee report.

Quantitative finance · Python

Jumps and Volatility in Developed and Emerging Markets

Fits Merton and Kou jump-diffusions, GARCH and GARCH-with-jumps models to daily index returns in 11 markets, 2005–2025, by maximum likelihood. It measures how much normal models understate tail risk and separates sudden jumps from waves of volatility.

View repository →

Machine learning · Python, PyTorch

Neural Network Forecasts in 11 Stock Markets

Walk-forward tests of LSTM networks in developed and emerging markets, 2013–2025. They cannot beat "always up" on market direction, even with rich inputs, but they are the best volatility forecasters tested, and volatility targeting with their forecasts cut drawdowns in 10 of 11 markets.
